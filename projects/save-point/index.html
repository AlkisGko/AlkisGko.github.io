01 / OVERVIEW

The idea behind the emails.

The service

SAVE POINT is a fictional gaming wishlist companion. Players save games they're interested in, and the service keeps an eye on offers so they don't have to.

The challenge is familiar to CRM teams: how do you stay useful after sign-up without filling someone's inbox with repetitive promotions? The answer begins with giving every email a reason to exist.

The approach

I designed a three-email journey around the customer's next useful action: a personal welcome, a prompt to create a wishlist, and a deal alert triggered by a genuine price improvement.

The concept uses customer and wishlist data, with a weekly price check for the final stage. An unchanged offer shouldn't produce another email. Only the Welcome email has been built so far; the other two stages are the planned next steps.
